## How credits work

Every AI-powered action in Seemodo costs credits. Your plan gives you a monthly credit allocation plus daily bonus credits.

### Daily credits

- **Free:** 5 credits per day (max 30/month total)
- **Pro & Business:** 5 daily credits on top of your 100 monthly credits (max 150/month total)

### Credit rollovers (Pro+)

On Pro and Business plans, unused monthly credits roll over to the next month. Free plan credits do not roll over.

### On-Demand Top-ups (Pro+)

Need more credits? Purchase additional credit packs anytime. Top-up credits stack with your monthly allocation and never expire.

### Credit tiers (Pro & Business)

Choose how many monthly credits you need when subscribing:

| Credits/month | Pro (monthly) | Business (monthly) |

| 400 | €100 | €200 |

Annual billing saves approximately 16% on all tiers.

## Managing your subscription

Access your billing settings via **Settings** → **Billing**:

- View current plan and credit balance
- See daily and monthly credit usage
- Upgrade or downgrade your plan
- Purchase credit top-ups
- Access invoices via the billing portal
- Manage payment methods

## Credit balance display

Your credit balance is always visible in the top header bar. Click it to open the account modal with:

- Current balance breakdown (monthly + daily + top-up)
- Usage history with timestamps and action types
- Quick link to buy more credits
