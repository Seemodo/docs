# Credits & Plans

Seemodo uses a credit system. Every AI action costs credits. Your plan determines how many monthly credits you receive and what features are available.

## Plans

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Free">
    **$0/month**
    
    - 100 monthly credits
    - HiFi UI generation
    - Wireframe generation
    - Export code
    - Screen Flows (max 3 screens)
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Standard">
    **$12/month** (or $9/month billed yearly — 25% off)
    
    - 420 monthly credits
    - Everything in Free, plus:
    - Design Review
    - Predictive Heatmap
    - Screen Flows (max 5 screens)
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Pro">
    **$22/month** (or $16.50/month billed yearly — 25% off)
    
    - 1,200 monthly credits
    - Everything in Standard, plus:
    - Unlimited Screen Flows
    - Image-to-Design
    - Section Edit
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Teams">
    **$31/month per user** (or $23.25/month billed yearly — 25% off)
    
    - 1,600 monthly credits per user
    - Everything in Pro, plus:
    - Shared Pool of Credits
    - Import Your Own Components
    - Custom Integration
    - Custom Onboarding
    - Seats and Roles
    - Priority Support
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## Credit costs per action

| Action | Credits |
|--------|---------|
| Generate HiFi screen | 6 |
| Generate Wireframe | 3 |
| Edit screen (AI edit) | 4 |
| Export code | 2 |

<Note>
Deep Design and Max Mode increase credit usage for higher quality output.
</Note>

## Buying extra credits

If you run out of monthly credits, purchase credit packs:

| Credits | Price |
|---------|-------|
| 360 | $18 |
| 720 | $34 |
| 1,440 | $63 |
| 2,880 | $118 |

Extra credits never expire and are used after your monthly allocation runs out.

## Checking your balance

Your credit balance is always visible in the top header:

⭐ **available** / **total**

Click the credit display to open the Account modal where you can:

- See your current balance (monthly + extra credits)
- View your credit usage history
- Buy credit packs
- Manage your subscription

## Credit usage history

The **Credits Usage** tab in Account Settings shows a detailed log:

- Every credit-consuming action with timestamp
- Which tool was used (HiFi Design, Wireframe, Edit, etc.)
- Credits remaining after each action
- Monthly resets

## Managing your subscription

From Account Settings → **Manage Plan**:

- View your current plan and billing cycle
- Upgrade or downgrade
- Switch between monthly and yearly billing
- Access invoices and billing history

## Upgrading

Click the **credit display** in the header or go to Account Settings → Manage Plan. Select the plan you want and complete the checkout.
